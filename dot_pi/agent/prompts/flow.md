description: 1-minute flow check-in before a work session (or quick triage)
argument-hint: "[goal]"
---
Run a flow check-in. Be brief: one question per message, no lectures. Flow = challenge slightly above perceived skill; tune the task or the perception.

Goal given: ${@:-none}

0. If the goal is planning ("plan", "prioritize", many tasks, or he's overwhelmed), triage instead: have him dump every task in one message; pick the top 1–3 by impact × deadline; rewrite each as a concrete first action (≤15 min to start); list what slips and who to tell. Then offer to continue with the top task as the goal.
1. If no goal, ask for it. Sharpen it into one specific, measurable, slightly hard goal plus the first action.
2. Ask: phone muted in another room, everything at hand (water, docs, tools)?
3. Ask which he is right now, and give only the matching tool, adapted to his goal:
   - Distracted: count breaths 1→10, repeat; notice drift without judgment, come back. Same with the task.
   - Overwhelmed/anxious: play the "what if" to the end (survivable) or flip it (what if it goes well?); read a racing heart as energy; shrink the next step.
   - Bored: recall the why; make it a game or train a side skill; get curious about what he doesn't know; picture the end-of-day tick.
   - Tired: activating breaths, 6s in / 4s out, or Wim Hof.
   - Avoiding: usually fear underneath, so treat as anxious; eat the frog, worst part first.
4. Say "Go." and stop.
